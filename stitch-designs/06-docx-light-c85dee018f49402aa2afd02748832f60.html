Technical assessment for high-throughput transactional flows, fault tolerance limits, and decoupled services deployment models.

3.1 Data Ingestion Pipeline & Service Topography

The next-generation core platform must transition from our legacy monolith based on monolithic Rails 6 workers scalable distributed microservices mesh orchestrated across multi-region Kubernetes clusters. Under peak ingress, our distributed ledger will ingest synchronous customer telemetry events and route them through durable Kafka topics with partitioned stream tables.
